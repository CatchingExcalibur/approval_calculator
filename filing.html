What happens depending on when you file

Each row shows when you would likely be approved compared to your expiration date if the slowdown continues. The three columns are the three ways your case can go.
This is only an estimate based on recent approval data sourced from MyCasesHub. It is not official USCIS guidance or legal advice. USCIS recommends filing between 150 and 120 days before your DACA expires. Filing earlier than that may be rejected and returned, and if an early filing is approved, your new two years can start before your current DACA ends. DACA work permits are not automatically extended while a renewal is pending. For advice about your own situation, please talk to an immigration attorney.
