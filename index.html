New two-track model (June 2026). USCIS recently changed how they approve cases. Instead of slowly working through a backlog, they are now approving most recent submissions within a few weeks, while a smaller group of older cases is still in extended background-check review. This calculator was rebuilt to reflect that. This pattern is only a couple of weeks old, so treat the estimates as a rough guide while it settles.
The date your case last had a status update before approval, which for most people is when they submitted their renewal.

Use this as a planning tool only, not a 100% guaranteed answer.
