USCIS is currently running two tracks at once: new applications are usually approved in about a month, while older applications are worked through month by month. Your estimate depends on which group your date falls in. Treat it as a rough guide, not a promise.
This is only an estimate based on recent approval data sourced from MyCasesHub. It is not official USCIS guidance and cannot guarantee an approval date. It is based on the average recent case, and your case can be different for many reasons, including RFEs, biometrics, background check status, country of origin, and case type. Please use this as a guide to help you plan, not as a 100% guaranteed answer.
